## GLOBAL SPECIFICATIONS (Apply to ALL 24 images)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (Mobile vertical only, portrait mode)
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920px minimum
- **Format:** PNG with transparency (RGBA)
- **Color Palette:** Charcoal (#0B0F19), Ivory (#F8F4EE), Champagne (#CBB89E), Soft Blush (#E7D4C8), Pure White (#FFFFFF), Warm Gray (#2F3A4B)
- **Critical:** NO text, words, numbers, or dates baked into any image
- **Critical:** Each image has reserved areas for text overlay (backgrounds designed for readable text placement)
